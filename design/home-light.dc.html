About CV Writing

The intersection of form and function

I'm an environmental scientist at Stantec. Before that I was a plant anatomist and physiologist, with a PhD from UConn and a postdoc at the University of Minnesota Duluth, studying how the structure of phloem, xylem and petioles shapes the way plants move carbon and water.

Currently, I work in environmental compliance and permitting.

Research, 2019–2025

About the research
  • Does drought season affect carbon transport in crop plants?

    Comparing spring, summer and fall droughts in Prunus, Populus and Quercus, alongside work on Citrus phloem and citrus greening.

  • How do petioles limit or enhance the movement of water and sugar?

    Vascular arrangement and packing in petioles, and what they mean for leaf physiology.
